Frequently asked questions

The essentials before you choose your next bag.

How do I place an order?+

Choose a bag from the collection and select Order, or contact our WhatsApp concierge. We will confirm availability, answer your questions, and guide you through the next steps.

Which payment methods do you accept?+

Payment is arranged by direct bank transfer after your order details are confirmed with our team. Please request payment details through our official WhatsApp contact.

Where do you deliver?+

Realluxe is based in Awka, Anambra State. Delivery options for Awka and other locations in Nigeria can be confirmed with our team when you enquire.

How can I check a bag's availability or details?+

Message us on WhatsApp with the bag name. Our team can confirm current availability and provide the details you need before placing an order.

Can I get help choosing a bag?+

Of course. Tell our concierge what style or occasion you have in mind, and we will help you explore the available collection.
